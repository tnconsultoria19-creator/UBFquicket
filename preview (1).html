Environment and work pathways 2026–2027 pilot

Sustainable Honeybush Programme

A planned community-based pilot combining sustainable Honeybush harvesting education, practical skills and fynbos conservation, with a focus on creating pathways for unemployed stewards.

The work plan describes an intake target of 25 unemployed stewards, with community mobilisation across Grassy Park and Lotus River. The timeline is phased:

  • October–November 2026: mobilisation and steward selection.
  • November–December 2026: foundational learning on sustainable harvesting.
  • January–February 2027: planned practical training and field expeditions.
  • March–April 2027: community bio-education and market-pathway activities.

Training dates, eligibility and selection are subject to programme arrangements and available places. Sending an expression of interest does not guarantee selection.

Applicants may be contacted for further information if their application is relevant to the intake process.

About UBF

Who we are

Unity Bridge Foundation (U.B.F.) South Africa is a Cape Town-based non-profit organisation. We work to connect local talent, opportunity and enterprise by helping small and medium-sized businesses grow and create opportunities for unemployed graduates and young people.

Our work also aims to cultivate ethical community and economic leadership and support environmental and economic pathways to work, dignity and shared prosperity.

What we focus on

Small business professionalisation

Practical planning, digital presence, business operations and access to appropriate support.

Skills and work pathways

Helping local SMEs strengthen their capacity to grow and engage talent responsibly.

Community and environmental learning

Supporting community participation and sustainable approaches to local environmental resources.

Practical support for people, businesses and communities

Our approach

Community-led, practical and partnership-based

The approach is designed around local needs and the scope of each initiative, rather than a one-size-fits-all promise.

01

Listen and identify needs

Engage participants, SMEs and community stakeholders to understand the practical need.

02

Plan the support

Set out the activity, timeframes, participation requirements and relevant partners.

03

Build practical capability

Use applied learning and programme activities relevant to the agreed initiative.

04

Review next steps

Communicate progress and identify realistic follow-on opportunities with relevant partners.

Our commitments

We aim to communicate requirements clearly, treat applicants respectfully, handle personal information carefully and avoid promising outcomes that depend on external decisions or available resources.

Privacy and information

Privacy Policy

Last reviewed: 9 October 2026. This draft should be checked against UBF’s actual operations and service-provider contracts before publication.

Unity Bridge Foundation South Africa (“UBF”, “we”, “us”) is a Cape Town-based non-profit organisation. This notice explains how we may handle personal information when you visit this website, ask a question, express interest in a programme or discuss a partnership. UBF should be contacted if you need a copy of this notice in another format.

Information we may collect

Depending on your interaction, this may include your name, email address, telephone number, community or suburb, business name or stage, information you include in a message, programme application details, communication preferences and correspondence. The website’s hosting and third-party asset providers may also process technical information such as IP address, browser/device details, requested resources and security logs.

Why we use it

  • To respond to enquiries and expressions of interest.
  • To administer applications, communicate selection steps and coordinate programme activities.
  • To work with relevant delivery partners and, where necessary, prepare programme monitoring or reporting.
  • To communicate optional UBF updates where permitted and where you have chosen to receive them.
  • To maintain website security, address misuse and meet legal obligations.

When information may be shared

UBF may share the minimum information reasonably needed with service providers who help host or maintain the website, support email and communications, or provide technical services. Depending on the initiative and the steps required, relevant information may also be shared with programme delivery partners, training providers, venues, referral or prospective host organisations, funders or reporting bodies, or public authorities where this is necessary and lawful. This may include sharing information with a third party to assess an application, coordinate activities, verify participation or report on programme delivery. We do not state that every listed category receives information for every programme; access should be limited to the purpose involved.

Website hosting and third-party services

This page loads certain fonts, icons, stylesheets and images from third-party services. When your browser requests these resources, those providers may receive technical information. The website repository does not identify the final hosting configuration or all provider contracts; UBF should confirm the actual host, operators, data locations and access arrangements before launch. Some providers may process information outside South Africa. Where applicable, UBF must assess and put in place the safeguards required by POPIA.

Lawful processing and choice

UBF should process personal information only for a defined, lawful purpose and on a basis permitted by law, such as taking steps at your request, performing a relevant arrangement, meeting a legal duty, protecting a legitimate interest where applicable or obtaining consent when required. You can decline optional marketing updates without affecting your programme enquiry. If you withdraw consent, this does not make earlier lawful processing unlawful, and UBF may need to retain some records for legal or administrative reasons.

Retention and security

Information should be kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, subject to applicable legal, contractual, reporting and audit requirements. UBF should use appropriate organisational and technical safeguards and restrict access to people who need the information for their roles. No website or transmission method can be represented as completely secure.

Your rights

Subject to applicable law and any relevant limits, you may ask whether UBF holds information about you, request access, request correction or deletion, object to certain processing, or withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. You may also opt out of direct marketing. Use the Data rights request page or email admin@unitybridge-foundation.org. UBF may need to verify your identity before acting.

Children’s information

Please do not submit personal information about another person, or information about a person under 18, through these forms without authority to do so. If an under-18 applicant wishes to participate, contact UBF first so that the appropriate consent and programme arrangements can be confirmed.

Complaints and contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact admin@unitybridge-foundation.org. You may also contact the South African Information Regulator through its official website: inforegulator.org.za.

South Africa

POPIA and data protection

How UBF intends to handle personal information responsibly.

The Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA) regulates the processing of personal information in South Africa. A website policy is only one part of responsible data handling; UBF must also make sure its actual practices, contracts, staff access and records meet applicable requirements.

Our intended approach

  • Collect information that is relevant to the stated enquiry or programme.
  • Explain the purpose of collection and the categories of recipients where information may be shared.
  • Use appropriate access controls and safeguards.
  • Keep records only as long as required for their purpose and applicable obligations.
  • Respond to valid access, correction, deletion, objection and consent-withdrawal requests.
  • Handle security incidents and any required notifications in accordance with applicable law.

Information officer and requests

For privacy-related requests, contact admin@unitybridge-foundation.org. UBF should confirm and publish the designated Information Officer’s details and its internal process before launch. To submit a request, use the Data rights request form.

Third-party operators and programme partners

UBF’s website hosting provider and technical service providers may process information needed to operate, secure or maintain the website. Programme-related information may also need to be shared with relevant delivery or reporting partners. UBF should document these relationships, limit access and sharing to what is required, and confirm safeguards for any cross-border processing.

For the legislation and official guidance, visit the South African Government’s POPIA page and the Information Regulator.
