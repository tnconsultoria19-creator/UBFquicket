Our Strategies

Structured Programs

Comprehensive interventions providing high-quality infrastructure for formalisation and employment.

Track 01

Small Business Startup Program

A four-week November 2026 programme focused on practical business planning, market research, payments, digital presence and operations.

Track 02

The Accelerator

UBF aims to help small and medium-sized businesses strengthen their planning, operations and workforce practices so they can grow responsibly.

    Business plans, profiles, and pitch decks.

    CIPC & SARS registration and alignment.

Who is Unity Bridge Foundation SA?

Based in Grassy Park and Lotus River communities, City of Cape Town.

Established and founded in January 2025.

An emerging-NPO evolving through strategic partnerships to respond to the needs of our residents.

"UBF SA exists to bridge the gap between talent, opportunity, small businesses, and the environment."

Vision and Mission

Sustainable SDGs

Empowering young people and job seekers through initiatives that uphold Global Sustainable Development Goals.

Green Economy

Positioning work seekers and small businesses within the future Green Economy of South Africa.

Ethical Leadership

Cultivating ethical community and economic leadership to protect agricultural and environmental heritage.

Creating environmentally responsible and sustainable pathways to work, dignity, and shared prosperity.

Problem Statement

Limited Connection

Lack of connection to Fynbos conservation areas (Princess Vlei, Rondevlei) among youth and unemployed.

Knowledge Gap

Limited awareness of Sustainable Harvesting Guidelines for Honeybush species in the Cape Metro.

Heritage Loss

Loss of agricultural and environmental heritage and lack of economic opportunity.

Reconnecting community and economic opportunity to environmental heritage.

Privacy Policy

Last reviewed: 9 October 2026. Draft wording; verify against UBF’s actual operations before launch.

Unity Bridge Foundation South Africa (“UBF”, “we”, “us”) is a Cape Town-based non-profit organisation. This notice explains how we may handle personal information when you visit this site, ask a question, express interest in a programme or discuss a partnership.

Information we may collect

Depending on your interaction, this may include your name, email address, telephone number, community or suburb, business name, programme interest and messages. The website host and third-party resource providers may also process technical information needed to deliver and secure the site.

How we use information

  • Respond to questions and expressions of interest.
  • Coordinate applications and relevant programme steps.
  • Communicate with applicants and partners about activities.
  • Maintain security and meet applicable obligations.
  • Send optional updates only where permitted by law and where required consent has been obtained.

Third-party operators and programme partners

The website is hosted and maintained using third-party infrastructure. Technical operators may process technical data or access logs needed to provide, troubleshoot and secure the site. UBF may also share relevant applicant information with delivery partners, training providers, venues, referral organisations, funders or reporting bodies where necessary and lawful to assess applications, coordinate activities or report on programme delivery. Sharing should be limited to the relevant purpose.

External resources and cross-border processing

This HTML loads fonts, icons, stylesheets and images from third-party services. A provider may receive technical information when your browser requests those resources. The actual host, provider contracts, processing locations and settings must be confirmed before launch. If personal information is processed or transferred outside South Africa, UBF must assess and apply the safeguards required by POPIA.

Retention and security

Information should be retained only as long as reasonably necessary for its purpose, subject to legal, reporting and audit requirements. UBF should use appropriate safeguards and restrict access to people who need the information for their role. No online system can be described as completely secure.

Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to information, ask for correction or deletion, object to certain processing, withdraw consent where applicable, or ask that optional marketing stop. Use the data rights request page or email admin@unitybridge-foundation.org. UBF may need reasonable identity verification.

Contact and complaints

For privacy requests, contact admin@unitybridge-foundation.org. You may also contact the South African Information Regulator at inforegulator.org.za.

POPIA and data protection

The Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 regulates personal information processing in South Africa. This page is one part of responsible data handling; it does not itself certify operational compliance.

Our intended approach

  • Collect information relevant to the stated enquiry or programme.
  • Explain collection purposes and categories of recipients.
  • Use appropriate safeguards and limit access.
  • Keep information only as long as required for its purpose and applicable obligations.
  • Respond to valid access, correction, deletion, objection and consent-withdrawal requests.
  • Handle security incidents and any required notifications in line with applicable law.
